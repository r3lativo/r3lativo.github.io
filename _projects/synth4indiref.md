A data synthesis pipeline developed as part of the [Frame of Reference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09365) project. It algorithmically generates complex situated dialogue scenarios — involving spatial and temporal relational references — and produces natural language dialogues along with challenging comprehension questions as a proxy for grounding ability.

The goal is to create training and evaluation data for models that need to track shared context across extended conversations.
